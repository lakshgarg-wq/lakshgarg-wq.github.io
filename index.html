Intent-based prospecting · Lead scoring

Signal platform for a services firm

Ten trackers (regulatory filings, public tenders, M&A and IPO news, stock-exchange disclosures) merged into one scored list of accounts across the UAE, Europe, the US and India. Each account comes with a why now attached, including a dated clock of compliance deadlines.

Outcome: a refreshable, ranked target list. It also showed that under half of the list was reachable in the core market, which moved where effort went.

Personalised outreach at scale · Human in the loop

AI research-and-draft engine with quality gates

For each account, sub-agents research the company, pick the best-fit service line and draft a first message. Before anything reaches me, every claim is checked against published proof, the CRM and the person's profile. A missing step stops the run.

Outcome: LinkedIn acceptance more than doubled after six weeks of testing message styles, and an inflated proof point was caught before it reached a prospect.

Funnel analytics

Finding the real bottleneck

Weekly audits of the CRM against the outreach log, stage by stage, from invite to accepted, first message, follow-up and reply.

Outcome: the data showed the constraint was follow-up volume, not message quality. That moved build effort away from better copy and onto the accept-to-first-message stage.

Data sourcing · Entity matching

GrantScope: a deeptech account database

Nine Spanish and EU grant programmes scraped, matched across sources and tagged into a deeptech core. It is rebuilt monthly in about 12 minutes and used to build scouting shortlists for ecosystem events.

Outcome: shortlists that took days of manual searching now take minutes. The same pattern now runs for EU tenders, PE deal flow and export-import buyer discovery.
