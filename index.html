Turn market signals into a sales pipeline

02

Make AI output trustworthy

AI is fast but confidently wrong. Most of my work is building the checks that catch it.

  • Added quality gates every outbound message must pass: claims checked against published proof, the CRM checked, the person's profile checked. Gates stop the work when a step is missing.
  • Caught and retracted an inflated proof point before it reached a prospect, by forcing published sources to outrank internal notes.
  • Keep a "judgment patterns" log: every reasoning flaw caught in a review becomes a rule so the same mistake isn't repeated.
Quality controlVerification designRisk awareness
03

Research rigorously, and kill my own ideas

For Indian stock-market research I built a backtesting system whose main job is to stop me fooling myself.

  • Built a single "honest engine" that prices every test with real share counts, real cash, trading costs and taxes. Moving to it cut earlier results that had been overstated 3–5×.
  • Ran a strategy through 10 rounds of attacks (luck, survivorship, liquidity, execution) and a walk-forward test of ~19,000 portfolios. It still beat the market out-of-sample.
  • Analysed my own 457 closed trades: 63% had peaked well above the eventual sell price. A simple take-profit rule would have beaten my actual exits, while every stop-loss rule did worse.

How I work

The habits that show up across everything above.

A number behind every claim

If it can't be measured, it's an opinion, and I label it as one.

Decisions are written down

One log is the final word, so a new teammate (or a new AI session) picks up exactly where the last one stopped.

Find the real bottleneck

When outreach stalled, the data showed the problem was follow-up volume, not message quality, so that's what got fixed.

Attack my own work first

Every idea gets stress-tested before it gets money, time or reputation.
