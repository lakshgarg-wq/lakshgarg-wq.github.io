Any model, day one

01The problem

Every AI model starts from zero about me, and each one quietly builds its own picture of who I am. I wanted one picture that I own and can check.

02Collection

My history with AI tools and the records of my working sessions are gathered into one corpus.

03Extraction

Instead of summarising, the system looks for patterns: what I repeat, what I refuse, how I decide, and where what I say differs from what I do.

04Evidence, not opinion

Every finding is backed by evidence and can change as new evidence arrives. What different AI models have concluded about me is compared, and I correct it where it's wrong.

05Output

A portable profile any model can load. It covers how I work, how I think and what I'm focused on now, so a new session behaves like a chief of staff who already knows me.

Built to be token-efficient

  • Cheaper models do the bulk mining, and the strongest model is kept for synthesis.
  • The finished profile is compact and loads in one step, not a pile of history.
