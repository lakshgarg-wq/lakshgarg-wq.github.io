Indian equity market trading intelligence

A personal research system for Indian equities. It ingests exchange data every day, parses company documents the moment they are published, and tests trading ideas on an engine built not to flatter them.

01A daily data pipeline

Every trading day the system downloads the exchange's public reports, structures them and loads them into a local database. The data is checked before anything downstream uses it.

02Signals, not noise

From that data it ranks stocks by conviction, flags risk, and shows which names keep coming up, not just today's.

03Automated document parsing

Company filings and earnings-call transcripts are parsed automatically as soon as they are published. The results feed a dashboard of critical highlights: what changed, what was promised and what looks like a red flag.

04A deep-dive page per stock

Each stock has its own page with scores and a private investment journal. Theses, checkpoints and notes accumulate, so the page gets more useful every quarter.

05Backtesting that doesn't lie

One engine prices every test the way real trading would. Every strategy has to survive hard attempts to break it before any result is believed.

06Weekly audit and improvement

Every mistake, mine or the AI's, is logged and reviewed weekly, and the system gets stricter each time.
