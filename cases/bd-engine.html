An AI-run business development engine

A system that finds companies with a reason to buy, researches them, and prepares a personalised first message. A person approves every message before it is sent.

01The task

Build a business development pipeline for a services firm across the UAE, Europe, the US and India, starting from no list and no shared method.

02Before

  • Prospects were picked by hand, with no signal for why now.
  • Research lived in people's heads, and each message started from scratch.
  • No shared record of what had been tried, decided or learned.

03Who does what

The AI does

  • Watches 10 sources: regulatory filings, public tenders, M&A and IPO news, stock-exchange disclosures.
  • Merges them into one scored list of accounts.
  • Researches each account and maps its pain to the best-fit service line.
  • Drafts the first message, checks every claim against published proof, and checks the CRM and the person's profile.
  • Runs as supervised sub-agents: cheaper models do tightly specified work, a stronger model reviews.

I do

  • Decide which markets and segments to go after.
  • Approve or rewrite every message before it is sent.
  • Hold the conversations and relationships.
  • Log each decision so the system and the team stay in sync.

04Result

  • A ranked, refreshable pipeline of 4,200 accounts. Only about 1,800 of them were reachable in the core market, which reset where effort went.
  • LinkedIn acceptance rose from about 18% to about 42% in six weeks of testing message styles.
  • A dated "regulatory clock" of compliance deadlines tells the team when each account is most likely to need help.
  • An inflated proof point was caught and retracted before it reached a prospect.

What broke, and what I learned

At first I kept improving targeting and message quality. The data showed the real bottleneck was elsewhere: accepted connections waiting too long for a first message, and too few follow-ups. Reply rates were normal for cold outreach. So the next design work went into follow-up volume, not better copy. The lesson I took: measure the whole funnel before optimising the step you enjoy.
