01The problem

A broker shows profit and loss. It doesn't show behaviour: why I bought, why I sold, and whether my habits helped or hurt.

02A ledger of every decision

Trade files spanning several years are rebuilt into a first-in-first-out ledger. Every lot is classified as closed, partial or open, and every buy and sell is linked to the market trend at that moment.

03Behaviour tags

Each closed trade is tagged with the pattern it shows, such as shaken out early, whipsawed, right on, held through, or held too long. An outlier lab picks out the trades that made or broke a period.

04Trader personas

My history is split into eras of equal trade counts, and each era gets its own trader persona. A separate layer then explains what changed between eras and which habit shifted.

05Replaying my exits

My real entries are replayed under systematic exit rules to test discipline against discretion. The results feed the rules I now trade by.
