The research and audit loop: agents propose, people challenge, the system improves

A workflow where an agent's research is handed to a person whose job is to test it, every verdict is recorded, and the whole system is audited and improved every month.

01The problem

Agent research reads just as confidently when it is wrong as when it is right. The real risk isn't a wrong fact. It's the decisions and assumptions an agent makes quietly along the way: why this account, why this person, why this angle.

02The packet makes assumptions visible

My agent produces a research packet for each account and lists the decisions and assumptions it made, so they can be tested rather than trusted.

03A second agent, steered by a person

The packet goes to an analyst who reports to me. The analyst runs deeper research with their own AI agent, focused on those assumptions, confirms or rejects each one and adds human feedback: what the model couldn't see or got wrong. The analyst works to a written standard, so every return comes back in the same shape.

04Recording the change

Each verdict is recorded. A rejected assumption becomes a rule the agent reads on its next run, so the same mistake doesn't come back.

05Feedback from what is actually sent

What I actually send, and how it lands, flows back into the same loop. Over time the drafts move closer to my own judgment.

06Monthly audit of the system

Once a month the whole workflow is audited stage by stage: data freshness, log completeness and where accounts stall. The findings change how the system works, not just its output. One audit showed the constraint was follow-up volume, not message quality, so the build effort moved there.
