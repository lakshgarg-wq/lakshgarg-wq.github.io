01The problem

Buying signals were scattered across feeds such as filings, tenders, deals, hiring and news. Each had its own format and its own idea of what counts as important, so there was no single ranked view of the market.

02Collection

Independent trackers watch the market, and the daily queue is only ever fed fresh data.

03One view per account

Every source lands in one place, grouped by account. What used to be scattered feeds is now one picture of each account.

04Priority

The market is sorted into Hot, Warm and Cold, so each day starts with the few accounts worth approaching now. A headline that sounds good is not enough to make an account hot.

05Applied across four geographies

The same design runs for the EU, the GCC, India and the US. Each region has its own sources and its own morning brief. One system serves four markets.

06Feeding outreach

Sentinel is the starting point for the daily outbound engine, and results flow back into it. Read more about that engine here.
