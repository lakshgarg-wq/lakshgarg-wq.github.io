GrantScope: who actually wins deeptech grants

A database of every company that won a Spanish or EU deeptech grant, rebuilt each month from public sources and used for startup scouting and account discovery.

01The problem

Grant results were spread across nine national, regional and EU programmes, each with its own site, format and naming. Building a shortlist meant days of manual searching.

02Collection

Each programme is collected on a monthly cycle, including one source with more than 400,000 rows. A full refresh takes about 12 minutes.

03Entity matching

The same company appears under different spellings and IDs across sources. The system matches them into one record, which is the hard part.

04Classification

Programmes that genuinely signal deeptech are written down as rules. That produces a deeptech core of about 1,600 companies, out of more than 22,000.

05Use

Shortlists for ecosystem events now take minutes instead of days. The same pattern runs for EU tenders, private-equity deal flow and export-import buyer discovery.

Built to be token-efficient

  • Collection and matching are plain code, with no model tokens per row.
  • Judgment calls are written as rules once, instead of being asked of a model every time.
