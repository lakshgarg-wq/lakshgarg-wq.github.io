GrantScope: who actually wins deeptech grants

A database of every company that won a Spanish or EU deeptech grant, rebuilt each month from public sources, for startup scouting and lead generation.

01The task

Build a scouting list of Spanish deeptech companies for ecosystem events and business development. No single source had this.

02Before

  • Grant results were spread across nine programmes, each with its own site, format and naming.
  • Building a shortlist meant days of manual searching and spreadsheets that were out of date at once.

03Who does what

The AI does

  • Scrapes nine national, regional and EU programmes, including one source with 429,000+ rows.
  • Matches the same company across sources despite different spellings.
  • Tags a 1,633-company deeptech core and flags which companies are commercial.
  • Exports filtered lists and feeds a dashboard.

I do

  • Choose which programmes count as a "deeptech" signal.
  • Check samples by hand before a list is used.
  • Turn lists into shortlists for specific events and conversations.

04Result

  • A monthly-refreshable database in place of manual research.
  • Deeptech shortlists for Spanish startup events, built in minutes.
  • The same pattern was reused for EU tenders, UAE disclosures, M&A deal flow and export-import buyer discovery.

What broke, and what I learned

The hard part was not scraping. It was deciding when two records are the same company, and which programmes really signal deeptech. Those are judgment calls, so they are written down as rules and checked by hand, not left to the model.
