01The problem

The firm had more than a thousand existing client accounts and almost nothing about them beyond a name. Researching each one by hand was impossible, and running an AI agent per account would have been slow and expensive.

02Enrichment at scale

The pipeline enriches the whole client list from public sources, without anyone researching a thousand companies by hand.

03Cost under control

The full list is processed at a fraction of what per-account AI research would cost, and effort goes where an opportunity is plausible.

04Provenance on every fact

Every fact carries its source. Because these packets travel to account owners, nothing unsourced is allowed through.

05From facts to pipeline

The output is a table of facts per account that maps each client to the services they don't buy yet. It becomes a ranked pipeline of upsell opportunities, routed to the people who own those relationships.
