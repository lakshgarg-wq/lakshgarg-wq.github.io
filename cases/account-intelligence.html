Account intelligence: finding upsell in 1,000+ client accounts

A pipeline that turns a bare list of more than a thousand existing client accounts into sourced facts, and those facts into new upsell opportunities.

01The problem

The firm had more than a thousand existing client accounts and almost nothing about them beyond a name. Researching each one by hand was impossible, and running an AI agent per account would have been slow and expensive.

02Fetch once, filter many

Instead of looking up a thousand companies one at a time, the pipeline downloads large public sources once and filters them by the client list. Work that would take a thousand lookups becomes one join.

03Gates before cost

A cheap pass runs on every account. Only the accounts that survive it get the expensive fields, so effort goes where an opportunity is plausible.

04Tiered reading

Most facts are read with no model at all, from structured data on the page. Where reading is needed, a small local model works on pre-trimmed text. The strongest model only checks samples and triages failures. It never reads the full volume.

05Provenance on every fact

Each extracted fact carries its source, the date it was retrieved and whether it was stated or inferred. Because these packets travel to account owners, nothing unlabeled is allowed through.

06From facts to pipeline

The output is a table of facts per account that maps each client to the services they don't buy yet. It becomes a ranked pipeline of upsell opportunities, routed to the people who own those relationships.

Built to be token-efficient

  • Fetching, reading and navigating are three separate jobs. Fetching is free code, reading is tiered, and navigation is built into the URLs rather than left to an agent loop.
  • The default reader costs nothing to run. Paid models are the exception, not the path.
